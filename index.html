About Our Studio

Crafting visual heirlooms in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh with passion, precision, and reverence for tradition.

Founder & Principal Director

Rohan Varma & Creative Collective

With over 8 years of experience documenting grand South Indian weddings across Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Vijayawada, and Hyderabad, our studio was born from a love for natural light, human emotion, and timeless composition.

We believe wedding photographs shouldn't look staged or dated after five years. Instead, we combine editorial portraiture with honest documentary moments—so decades later, you can still feel the warmth of the sacred fire and the joy of your loved ones.
